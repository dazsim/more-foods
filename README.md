# More Foods

More food types for Minecraft.

Adds tomatoes, lettuce, onions, cabbage, spinach, kale, garlic, leeks, peas, haricot beans, parsnips, turnips and swedes as crops (seeds drop from grass), kitchen tools (knife, pot, mixing bowl, pestle and mortar, frying pan, juicer, mincer) and a long list of foods made with them: breads, sandwiches, burgers, pizza, stews, roast dinners, a cooked breakfast and more. Cows, pigs, chickens and sheep killed by a player drop extra bits for cooking.

Built for Minecraft 26.1 on NeoForge and Fabric. With Much More Storage installed, the assembly board also sits on its cupboards and worktops.

## Cookbook

With Simple Books installed (optional, not needed to play) there is a More Foods Cookbook: a step by step guide to every recipe, tool and crop. Craft it from a book and any More Foods seed, or get one the first time you pick up a More Foods seed or crop. Without Simple Books the book files are simply unused and the recipe and reward do not load. The pages live in `assets/morefood/simplebooks/`.

The crop models, blockstates and crop textures are generated by `tools/cropmodels.py` (Python 3 with Pillow). Run it from the repo root after changing a shape.
